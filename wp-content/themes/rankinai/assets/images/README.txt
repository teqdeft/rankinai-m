RankinAI — image briefs
=======================

Every .svg in this folder is a placeholder. Each one is labelled with the shot
it is standing in for, and every one says PLACEHOLDER — DO NOT SHIP.

Replace with real photography before launch. Nothing here goes live.


TREATMENT — applies to every photograph on the site
---------------------------------------------------
Warm daylight. Soft contrast. Cream-leaning whites.
No teal-orange grade. No heavy vignette.
No borders, no rounded corners, no drop shadows — surfaces are separated by
1px hairlines only.

BANNED, by name, in the brand guidelines
----------------------------------------
Handshakes. Rising arrows. Glowing brains. Robots. Server rooms.
City skylines at dusk. Anyone pointing at a whiteboard.
Stock people of any kind, even temporarily — temporary stock survives to launch.
